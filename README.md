## How I learn

I learn best by building things and following my curiosity. My projects don't always follow a straight path, I often move between ideas and technologies as something sparks my interest.

Some experiments become more developed projects, while others simply help me understand an idea before I move on to something else.

I use practical projects to explore unfamiliar technologies, work through problems, debug issues and gradually turn concepts into something I can actually use.

My background in infrastructure and technical support also gives me an appreciation of the wider environment that software operates within, from operating systems and hardware through to troubleshooting, reliability and repeatable processes.
